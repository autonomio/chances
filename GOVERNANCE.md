# Governance

Chances is maintained by autonomio.

Repository maintainers decide issue priority, release timing, review requirements, and whether a contribution fits the project scope.

The mechanical rules — what blocks a merge and why — are not governance decisions and are not made here. They live in [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md), the constitution, and are enforced by the gates it names. A maintainer cannot wave a gate through; changing what is enforced means changing the law and the ruleset together, in a PR of its own.

## Decision Records

Material product decisions should be traceable through GitHub issues, pull requests, release notes, or docs updates.
